What it does in the game. ChadcoreMenu reads the game's memory and data files to draw its panels. For the actions you choose on its panels (equipping gear, moving items between bags, trading with an NPC, buying from a player's bazaar, setting bazaar prices, warping by Home Point or Survival Guide, answering NPC menus, running macros) it uses the game's own menus or sends the same requests the game itself sends. The server checks each of them as it always does. It does not automate play, play by itself, or give any in-game advantage beyond a different-looking interface. Even so, a bug in the addon could pick the wrong option. Check what you've chosen before confirming anything valuable, and turn a feature off (/cmenu <feature> off, or /cmenu off for all of it) if it misbehaves.

Your data. ChadcoreMenu does not connect to the internet and does not collect or send any of your information. Its settings, and the log files it writes to help with troubleshooting (chadcoremenu-survey.txt, chadcoremenu-errors.txt and the like), stay in its folder on your PC. Those logs can contain your character's name and linkshell members' names, so check them before you share them with anyone.
